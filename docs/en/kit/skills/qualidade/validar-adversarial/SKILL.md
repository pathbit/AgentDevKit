# Adversely Validate

[Português (Brasil)][reading-pt] · [English][reading-en] · [Español][reading-es]

Read the scope, change, and evidence. Prioritise hypotheses of probability and
impact failure. Consider invalid data, limits, permissions, absent states,
dependencies, and competition when applicable.

Test with authorized data and environment, prefer isolation and non-destructive
checks. Use independent review when available and authorized. Otherwise, do
second critical reading and report the limitation. Check the test output code,
including when filtering the output on a pipeline.
[review-safe](../../seguranca/revisar-seguranca/SKILL.md).

Report scenario, observation, impact, location, and confidence. Enter confirmed
failures, hypotheses, and pending tests. Restrict the conclusion to the observed
coverage and treat unproven as conditional impacts without exposure. Repeat the
relevant verification after correction.
