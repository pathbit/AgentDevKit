# Review code

[Português (Brasil)][reading-pt] · [English][reading-en] · [Español][reading-es]

Read the diff, contracts, accepted and relevant tests. Act in reading when you
are the [reviewer](../../../agents/revisor/AGENT.md). Check the coverage before
stating that a test is missing and preserves other people's changes.

Prioritise concrete defects and change regressions. Consider data, competition,
faults, security and deployment at risk.
[validation-adversarial](../validar-adversarial/SKILL.md) when there is a
specific hypothesis to refute.

For each finding, report file, line, scenario, impact and evidence. Distinguish
hypotheses of facts and limit the statement to the cases shown. Tell what you
have consulted, what you have executed and the limits of the review.
