# Next steps

[Português (Brasil)][reading-pt] · [English][reading-en] · [Español][reading-es]

Complete answers about work with result, to-dos and next applicable action,
using the language chosen in the project. On chat or screen, finish with **[
Next step ]** e a ação/comando; use **[ Next step ]** in English or **[ Next
passo ]** in Spanish. Use a skill [Next
steps](../../skills/projeto/proximos-passos/SKILL.md) when you need to determine
the phase or its prerequisites. Do not repeat completed steps or confuse
installation of instructions with product approval or test execution.
