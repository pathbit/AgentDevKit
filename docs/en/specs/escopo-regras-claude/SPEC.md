# Scope of rules at Claude Code

[Português (Brasil)][reading-pt] · [English][reading-en] · [Español][reading-es]

## Objective

Avoid conditional hub rules being charged globally on Claude and maintaining
human rules, Git index and existing settings intact.

## Functional requirements

- `always_on` can be generated for `.claude/rules` as a global rule.
- `glob` requires Claude scope `paths` explicit and syntactically supported to
  generate rule by paths. Without this metadata, keep reading explicit. Even
  with it, do not assume equivalence of the glob between customers.
- `manual` e `model_decision` They don't have proven automatic equivalents in
  Claude.
- The same decision applies to automatic generations and explicit native
  entries. Invalid or ambiguous metadata should not result in a global rule.
- Preserve human rules and incompatible or ambiguous frontmatter, including with
  `--force`. Identity of content does not prove property.
- When migrating an old reference, preserve paths without proven property or
  integrity and block the removal of tracked or staged content. Bytes hash alone
  does not prove property or preservation of metadata: identical replacements
  and mode or attributes changes can be human. Without sufficient proof,
  preserve and request revision. An ancient human reference, modified or without
  property that can still be loaded globally, must generate conflict: `--check`
  e JSON Claude's demarcation can preserve these rules and complete the
  selection, provided that he informs them that they may still be active and
  maintain the unapproved verification diagnostics until the situation is
  resolved.
- `init`, `update`, selection of agents, `sync` e `--check` must apply the same
  contract without changing Git configuration, index or project ignores. This
  includes direct execution of the sync script: a `--check` You must not declare
  success if Claude's preserved rules can still be active, a border that
  prevents you from inspecting. `.claude/rules` It should also be flagged when
  the catalog contains only global rules. The inspection should consider
  subfolders that Claude loads even when their names usually indicate cache; do
  not discard legacy rule records without resolving their preservation and
  possible loading.

## Non-functional requirements

Keep notes small and without symbolic links. `--check` is read only. Errors must
identify the path and action required without exposing private content. Tests
and fixtures are versioned; their outputs are not.

## Scope and limits

The change covers fate, frontmatter, manifesto, state, catalog and migration of
Claude rules. Copilot policy must be preserved and tested. Do not assume
equivalence of globs between Claude and Antigravity or that a Markdown link is
automatically loaded. It does not promise atomicity under concurrent editing:
this limitation is treated in the [conservative
recovery](../recuperacao-conservadora/SPEC.md).

## Technical restrictions

Claude plays `paths` and can carry a rule without scope when the YAML is
invalid. Use validated serialization and fail before changing destination in
unsupported cases. `.pbai4devs/scripts/lib/claude-rule-scope.mjs` accepts a
subset of YAML and is integrated into the local sync. The implementation and its
migration still depend on the checks pending below, including competition and
Windows.

## Acceptance and verification criteria

The proposed evidence remains in `.agent-tests/escopo-regras-claude/`; all
criteria are pending and require independent implementation and review.

- **AC-001 — hanging:** With Node 24+, run the planner and integration tests.
  YAML invalid synthetics, triggers and metadata do not extend scope, including
  in lines `nativo` Manual references, by decision or without `paths` do not
  enter into `.claude/rules`. Evidence: `planejamento.log`.
- **AC-002 — hanging:** With Git and fixtures with human content and staged, run
  `init`, `update`, `agents` e `sync` and compare human data, index,
  configuration, `.git/info/exclude` and ignore byte a byte. Include identical
  or mixed human content, staged change and deletion, links and aliases.
  Evidence: `preservacao.log`.
- **AC-003 — hanging:** In fixtures with their own, human, edited and staged
  references, migration tests remove only their own, intact and untraced
  references, with proven property and metadata; other cases are preserved and
  flagged, including after demarcating Claude. Include identical byte
  replacement and mode and attributes changes. Check destinations, status,
  catalog and manifest. Evidence: `migracao.log`.
- **AC-004 — hanging:** With Claude available and fixture isolated, register
  `InstructionsLoaded` for reference generated by the installer: it loads in the
  corresponding file, without pressing at the beginning or outside the scope.
  Check the source read separately. Evidence: `claude.json`.
- **AC-005 — hanging:** With Claude, Copilot and Antigravity selections, the
  tests of adapters and mixed fixtures do not broaden scope or declare unproven
  equivalence. Evidence: `clientes.log`.
- **AC-006 — hanging:** On macOS, Linux and Windows, run applicable fault tests
  and competition and register platform limitations. Assertive: no pending is
  counted as approved. Evidence: `plataformas.log`.

The tests `tests/claude-rule-scope.test.mjs` e
`tests/claude-rule-scope-integration.test.mjs` cover the planner and direct
sync; `tests/claude-rule-cli.test.mjs` Covers CLI entries. With Claude Code
2.1.322, a rule `glob` generated by the built-in sync was loaded only to the
corresponding file; the explicit reading of the source was checked in separate
session. This does not prove automatic reading of the source, the generation by
the installer, the other criteria or equivalence with Antigravity.
`InstructionsLoaded` recorded at the beginning both a legacy reference
`always_on` as a `model_decision`, both without `paths`. The reading events of
the sources appeared only when requested in separate session; absence of `Read`
The proof used sandbox in macOS, with a session by condition, and does not
demonstrate obedience or determinism.
